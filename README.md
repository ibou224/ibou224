### About

I started in data — building BI pipelines and administering databases — before moving fully into AI. That background still shapes how I work: I care about clean data, reproducibility, and shipping systems that don't break in production.

I just graduated from the AI & Computer Science program at Collège La Cité (Ottawa), where my capstone — a Vision OCR Accessibility Assistant — became the project I'm most proud of: a real DBNet+ / ResNet50 / TrOCR pipeline served through FastAPI and React, with measurable wins on latency and character error rate.

Beyond the code, I'm interested in **responsible AI** — I contributed to drafting an ethical AI charter for my college, aligned with the EU AI Act and the Canadian Charter. Building useful systems matters; building them responsibly matters more.

I'm fully bilingual (French / English) and based in the Ottawa–Gatineau region, open to Junior ML Engineer, Data Scientist or AI Engineer roles — with a strong focus on **Ottawa, Gatineau, Montréal and Québec**, including francophone-track opportunities.
